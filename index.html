What an episode is

You arrive by yacht. Natalia, the host, runs the night on a clock: a welcome, a challenge, time at the bar with Marco, confessionals in the booth off the west wing, and the Pairing, where someone stands up front and chooses. A bombshell can walk in at the hour. The confessionals come back at movie night on the big screen by the pool, and the season ends with a winning couple.

You play as your GTA character. Your voice is real; your face is not on camera. Confessionals are recorded and shown inside the villa, and the best ones are cut into clips for the show's TikTok.

Who we're looking for

  • 18 or older, no exceptions.
  • A PC with GTA V (Legacy) and FiveM installed, or willing to install FiveM before night one.
  • A microphone and a Discord account. You'll talk, a lot.
  • Free for the three episode nights, 8 PM Central, dates set with the cast in Discord.
  • Someone who commits to a bit. Flirts, schemes, has opinions in the confessional, and is decent to people when the cameras are off.
